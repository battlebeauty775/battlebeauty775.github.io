Red Light & Far-Infrared Technology

How Red LED & Infrared Light Protect Your Hair

Traditional styling tools bake the outer hair cuticle first. Red LED and far-infrared light work smarter—warming strands gently from the inside out so your hair stays hydrated, glossy, and resilient.

Inside-Out Moisture Lock

Red LED & Far-Infrared Benefits

Red light wavelengths and far-infrared energy penetrate directly into the inner hair cortex without scorching the delicate outer cuticle layer.

  • Seals natural moisture inside the hair shaft for lasting shine
  • Reduces frizz, static, and heat-induced dryness
  • Delivers smooth, sleek results in fewer passes
Smart Temperature Control

Why Professional Tools Reach 450°F

Professional infrared flat irons reach up to 450°F (232°C) specifically for licensed salon keratin and smoothing treatments—not everyday home styling.

  • Fine or color-treated hair: 280°F – 350°F
  • Medium to thick hair: 350°F – 390°F
  • 450°F: Reserved for professional salon keratin sealing protocols

HEAT SAFETY GUIDANCE: Always style on 100% dry hair, apply a heat protectant, and consult your licensed hairstylist or ask us during a free kiosk demo to choose the safest temperature setting for your hair type.
