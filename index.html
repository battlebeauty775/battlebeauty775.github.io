Infrared Red Light & 450°F Keratin Technology

Understand how far-infrared light protects internal hair moisture—and why 450°F is engineered specifically for licensed salon keratin sealing protocols.

Infrared & Red Light Therapy

Far-infrared waves warm the inner hair cortex gently from within rather than scorching the surface cuticle, locking in natural hydration and boosting daily elasticity.

Why 450°F Is For Salon Keratin Sealing

Professional flat irons reach 450°F (232°C) to thermally seal in-salon keratin and smoothing treatments. For daily home styling, lower infrared settings (280°F–390°F) deliver glass-like shine safely.

PROFESSIONAL HEAT NOTICE: 450°F is a professional-grade maximum temperature engineered for licensed salon keratin sealing protocols and is not recommended for routine daily home styling. Always ensure hair is 100% dry, apply thermal protection, and consult with your licensed hairstylist or the salon where you purchased your tool to determine the safest daily temperature setting for your specific hair type and chemical history.

AVISO PROFESIONAL: 450°F es una temperatura máxima profesional diseñada para sellado de queratina en salón y no se recomienda para uso diario en casa. Siempre aplique protector térmico sobre cabello 100% seco y consulte con su estilista profesional o el salón donde adquirió su herramienta para elegir la temperatura diaria adecuada para su tipo de cabello.

The Botanical Shield Routine

How to layer Trio Miracle Oil (12–18" arm's-length mist + warm blow-dry melt) and Moroccan Hair Serum for weightless silkiness.

01. Match Heat to Hair Density

Fine, color-treated, or post-keratin hair thrives between 280°F–350°F, while thick or coarse textures respond best to steady infrared heat at 350°F–390°F.

02. Never Press Damp Strands

Always dry hair 100% before using flat irons or styling combs to prevent internal steam damage to the hair shaft.

03. Seal With Cold & Botanicals

Finish every style with a cool-air pass and 1–2 light sprays of Trio Miracle Oil from 12–18 inches away to lock out humidity.
